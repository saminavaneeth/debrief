# 29 CFR 1910.178 — Powered industrial trucks (summary)

Paraphrased summary for reference inside Debrief. Always check the official regulation text for compliance decisions.

## Operator training — 1910.178(l)

Only trained and evaluated operators may drive a powered industrial truck. Refresher training is required after an operator is observed driving unsafely, is involved in an accident or near-miss, or when an evaluation shows the need. Each operator is re-evaluated at least once every three years.

## Traveling — 1910.178(n)

Operators slow down and sound the horn at cross aisles and other places where vision is obstructed, look in the direction of travel, and keep a clear view of the path of travel.

## Defective trucks — 1910.178(p)(1)

A truck found in need of repair, defective, or in any way unsafe is taken out of service until it has been restored to safe operating condition.

## Daily examination — 1910.178(q)(7)

Trucks are examined before being placed in service, at least daily (after each shift when used around the clock). Defects that affect safety are corrected before the truck is used.

## Applying it in Debrief

A near-miss involving a forklift notes the truck, the operator and whether the truck was removed from service. A known defect on a truck involved in a near miss raises the risk of the existing hazard. A near miss is also a trigger for refresher training under (l).
