# 29 CFR 1910.157 — Portable fire extinguishers (summary)

Paraphrased summary for reference inside Debrief. Always check the official regulation text for compliance decisions.

## Availability and access — 1910.157(c)(1)

Where the employer provides portable extinguishers for employee use, they must be mounted, located and identified so that they are readily accessible to employees without subjecting them to possible injury. An extinguisher blocked by stored material does not meet this requirement.

## Kept charged and operable — 1910.157(c)(4)

Extinguishers must be maintained in a fully charged and operable condition and kept in their designated places at all times except during use. A gauge reading in the red (undercharged or overcharged) range means the unit is not in operable condition.

## Monthly visual inspection — 1910.157(e)(2)

Portable extinguishers are visually inspected monthly.

## Annual maintenance — 1910.157(e)(3)

Each extinguisher receives a maintenance check at least annually. The date of the annual check is recorded and the record kept for one year after the last entry or for the life of the shell, whichever is less.

## Applying it in Debrief

A failed monthly inspection item for gauge or access is a fire protection hazard; the hazard cites the relevant paragraph above.
