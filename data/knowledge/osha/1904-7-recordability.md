# 29 CFR 1904.7 — General recording criteria (summary)

Paraphrased summary for reference inside Debrief. Always check the official regulation text for compliance decisions.

## When a work-related case is recordable — 1904.7(a)

A work-related injury or illness is recorded on the OSHA 300 log when it results in any of: death, days away from work, restricted work or transfer to another job, medical treatment beyond first aid, loss of consciousness, or a significant injury or illness diagnosed by a physician or other licensed health care professional.

## Medical treatment — 1904.7(b)(5)(i)

Medical treatment means managing and caring for a patient to combat a disease or disorder. It does not include visits solely for observation or counseling, diagnostic procedures such as X-rays, or first aid.

## First aid list — 1904.7(b)(5)(ii)

The following are first aid, and a case involving only these is not recordable on that basis. The list is complete; anything not on it is medical treatment.

- (A) Non-prescription medication at non-prescription strength
- (B) Tetanus immunizations
- (C) Cleaning, flushing or soaking wounds on the surface of the skin
- (D) Wound coverings such as bandages, gauze pads, butterfly bandages or Steri-Strips
- (E) Hot or cold therapy (for example, an ice pack)
- (F) Non-rigid means of support such as elastic bandages, wraps or non-rigid back belts
- (G) Temporary immobilization devices used while transporting a patient
- (H) Drilling a fingernail or toenail to relieve pressure, or draining fluid from a blister
- (I) Eye patches
- (J) Removing foreign bodies from the eye using only irrigation or a cotton swab
- (K) Removing splinters or foreign material from areas other than the eye by irrigation, tweezers, cotton swabs or similar simple means
- (L) Finger guards
- (M) Massages
- (N) Drinking fluids for relief of heat stress

## Restricted work and days away — 1904.7(b)(3), (b)(4)

Days away and restricted work are counted from the day after the injury. If the worker returns to full duty the same day, the case is not recordable on this basis.

## Applying it in Debrief

An injury treated only with items on the first-aid list, where the worker returns to normal duties, is classified as not recordable, citing 1904.7(b)(5)(ii) and the specific item used.
