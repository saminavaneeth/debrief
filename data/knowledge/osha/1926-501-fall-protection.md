# 29 CFR 1926.501 — Duty to have fall protection, construction (summary)

Paraphrased summary for reference inside Debrief. Always check the official regulation text for compliance decisions.

## Unprotected sides and edges — 1926.501(b)(1)

Each employee on a walking or working surface with an unprotected side or edge 6 feet or more above a lower level is protected by a guardrail system, a safety net system, or a personal fall arrest system.

## Leading edges — 1926.501(b)(2)

Employees constructing a leading edge 6 feet or more above a lower level are protected by guardrails, safety nets or personal fall arrest, unless the employer shows those are infeasible and follows a written fall protection plan.

## Applying it in Debrief

A contractor worker at a deck edge roughly 12 feet above the floor without tie-off or guardrail is a serious unsafe act under (b)(1). It is recorded as an observation and, when a contractor safety audit is open, as a high-severity audit finding citing 1926.501(b)(1).
