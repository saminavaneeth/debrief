# 29 CFR 1910.28 — Duty to have fall protection, general industry (summary)

Paraphrased summary for reference inside Debrief. Always check the official regulation text for compliance decisions.

## Unprotected sides and edges — 1910.28(b)(1)

In general industry, employees on a walking-working surface with an unprotected side or edge 4 feet or more above a lower level are protected by guardrails, safety nets, or personal fall protection systems such as fall arrest, restraint or positioning.

## Related: surface condition — 1910.22(a)

The companion general requirement in 1910.22(a) says walking-working surfaces are kept clean, orderly and sanitary; floors are kept free of hazards such as sharp objects, loose boards, corrosion, leaks, spills, snow and ice; and floors are kept dry where practical, with drainage, mats or dry standing places where wet processes are used.

## Applying it in Debrief

Fall exposures in general-industry work (maintenance, operations) use the 4-foot threshold; construction work uses 1926.501. Wet floors from leaks are surface-condition hazards and cite 1910.22(a).
