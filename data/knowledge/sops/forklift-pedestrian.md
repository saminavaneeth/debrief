# Forklift operation and pedestrian separation

Cedar Valley Manufacturing · EHS-SOP-04 · Applies to all powered industrial trucks at Plant 1 and Building A.

## Pre-use check

Operators check the truck at the start of every shift using the pre-shift checklist: mirrors, horn, reverse alarm, lights, brakes, forks, chains and leaks. Any defect that affects safe operation is reported to the shift lead before the truck is used.

## Removing a truck from service

A truck with a defect that affects visibility, braking, steering or load handling is tagged out with a red "Do Not Operate" tag and the key is removed. It stays out of service until maintenance repairs it and signs the tag. A cracked or missing mirror counts as a visibility defect.

## Pedestrian separation

- Pedestrians use the marked yellow lanes. Forklifts do not enter pedestrian lanes except at marked crossings.
- When reversing, operators look in the direction of travel, not only at the mirrors, and sound the horn at blind corners and dock doors.
- Operators stop and make eye contact before passing a pedestrian within 10 feet.
- Faded lane markings are reported as a hazard and repainted within 30 days.

## Near-miss reporting

Any event where a truck came close to striking a person, structure or another truck is reported as a near-miss incident the same shift, including the truck number, the operator and anyone involved. Repeated near misses in the same area trigger a review of traffic flow.

- Faded or missing lane markings → Repaint the pedestrian lane markings | owner: role:facilities | due: 30 days | keywords: faded, markings, lane lines
- Unsafe operator behaviour → Coach the operator and schedule refresher training | owner: role:site_manager | due: 7 days | keywords: speeding, horn, operator, unsafe driving
