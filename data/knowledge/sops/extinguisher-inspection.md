# Portable fire extinguisher monthly inspection

Cedar Valley Manufacturing · EHS-SOP-02 · Applies to all portable extinguishers.

## What to check on each unit

1. Gauge needle in the green range.
2. Safety pin and tamper seal in place.
3. Mounted in its marked location, visible and unobstructed (clear 3-foot zone in front).
4. No dents, corrosion, leaks or blocked nozzle.
5. Annual maintenance tag present and current.

## Recording

Each unit gets pass or fail on the monthly checklist with a note for any failure. The inspection record is completed the same day and the inspector initials the unit tag.

## Failures

- Gauge low or in the red: remove the unit from service and replace it with a spare the same day; send the unit for service.
- Blocked or obstructed: clear the obstruction immediately if possible; if the cause is recurring storage, record a hazard and notify the area lead.
- Every failed unit gets its own hazard record linked to the inspection.
