# Wet floors and housekeeping

Cedar Valley Manufacturing · EHS-SOP-05 · Applies to all walking surfaces.

## Wet floors

- Anyone who finds a wet floor puts out a wet-floor sign at once and reports it.
- Spills are cleaned immediately. A continuing leak is reported to maintenance so the source is fixed, not only the puddle.
- Areas with recurring water (ice machines, sinks, entrances) have absorbent mats and are checked at the start of each shift.

## Trip hazards

Hoses, cords and pallets are kept out of walkways. Damaged floor plates and dock plates are reported as hazards.

## Corrective action guidance

- Equipment leak causing a wet floor: maintenance repair within 3 days; place a sign and mat until repaired.
- No sign in place: brief the area on the wet-floor procedure; keep signs stocked at the location.
