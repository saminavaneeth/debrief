# Fall protection

Cedar Valley Manufacturing · EHS-SOP-07 · Applies to employees and contractors.

## Thresholds

- General industry work (maintenance, operations): fall protection at 4 feet or more above a lower level.
- Construction work, including the Plant 1 mezzanine expansion: fall protection at 6 feet or more above a lower level.

## Acceptable protection

Guardrails are the first choice. Where guardrails are not in place, workers use a full-body harness and lanyard tied off to an approved anchor, or work behind a safety net. Warning lines alone do not protect workers who are at the edge.

## Contractors

Contractors submit a fall protection plan before work at height starts and follow it on site. The contractor foreman is responsible for daily checks of anchors, harnesses and lanyards.

## Stop work

Anyone who sees a person exposed to a fall without protection stops the work, gets the person to a safe position, and informs the supervisor or contractor foreman. The event is recorded as an observation; for contractors it is also recorded as a finding on the open contractor safety audit.
