# Spill response

Cedar Valley Manufacturing · EHS-SOP-09 · Applies to oil, fuel and chemical releases at all sites.

## Small leaks and drips (under 5 gallons)

1. Identify the source and stop it if it is safe to do so (close an upstream valve, tighten a fitting, place a drip pan).
2. Contain with absorbent pads or socks; keep the material away from drains.
3. Clean up and bag used absorbent as oily waste.
4. Record the source as a hazard so the repair is tracked, even if the leak was stopped.

## Larger releases

Evacuate the immediate area, notify the shift lead and EHS, and use the spill kit at the nearest station. Releases that reach soil, drains or water are reported as environmental incidents.

## Tank farm

Valve and flange leaks at the tank farm are checked against the secondary containment. Any sheen on the containment floor is cleaned the same day. A leaking valve is tagged and scheduled for repair by maintenance within 3 days.

## Corrective action guidance

- Leaking valve or fitting: place drip pan and absorbent now; maintenance repair or replace within 3 days.
- Sheen in containment: clean the same day; inspect the source weekly until repaired.
